# Content Factory Orchestrator

## Purpose

Use this skill as the front door for a social content factory. Convert a brand goal, niche, product, founder voice, campaign, transcript, or raw idea into a coordinated Telegram, Instagram, and TikTok production plan.

## Factory Workflow

1. Define the operating context:
   - Business goal: awareness, trust, leads, launches, community, recruitment, sales enablement.
   - Audience: pain, sophistication level, objections, desired identity, buying stage.
   - Offer: what the content should make easier to understand, believe, or buy.
   - Brand voice: energy, taboo words, proof style, humor level, language mix.
   - Constraints: cadence, available footage, face/no-face, team capacity, approvals.

2. Build the content machine:
   - Pillars: authority, education, proof, opinion, behind the scenes, product, community.
   - Inputs: founder notes, sales calls, FAQs, case studies, testimonials, product demos, events, research, long videos.
   - Platform roles: Telegram for depth and trust, Instagram for visual packaging and saves, TikTok for reach and raw idea testing.
   - Output mix: long posts, short posts, Reels, TikToks, carousels, stories, captions, comment prompts, repost variants.

3. Route specialist work:
   - Use `$telegram-channel-writer` for posts, series, launch notes, and community trust.
   - Use `$instagram-reels-carousel-factory` for Reels, carousels, covers, captions, and story frames.
   - Use `$tiktok-shortform-script-lab` for TikTok-native concepts and scripts.
   - Use `$hook-retention-lab` when openings, watch time, or scroll-stopping power are weak.
   - Use `$content-repurposer` to turn one source into platform-native assets.
   - Use `$short-video-storyboard` when production needs shots, VO, on-screen text, and editing notes.
   - Use `$caption-subtitle-localization` for subtitles, captions, translations, and language variants.
   - Use `$content-calendar-ops` for calendar, status, batching, and publishing rhythm.
   - Use `$social-publishing-qa` before publishing or handing assets to a team.
   - Use `$trend-research-scout` for current trend, format, sound, and competitor research.
   - Use `$brand-voice-keeper` to preserve a consistent voice across all outputs.

## Output Standard

Prefer a compact production table with:

`date | platform | pillar | source | format | hook | asset | CTA | owner | status | notes`

For campaign planning, also include:

`goal | audience stage | key promise | proof | objection handled | metric to watch`

## Quality Rules

- Make each asset platform-native; do not copy-paste the same text across platforms.
- Anchor claims in supplied facts. Browse or ask for sources before using current stats, laws, prices, platform rules, or trend claims.
- Prefer one clear job per post: teach, reframe, prove, invite, sell, or start a conversation.
- Build repeatable batches: one research source should become multiple posts, not a single isolated output.
- Keep approvals visible: draft, needs source, needs footage, design, scheduled, published, repurpose.
- Protect brand trust: no fake metrics, fake testimonials, invented client names, or unverifiable trend claims.
