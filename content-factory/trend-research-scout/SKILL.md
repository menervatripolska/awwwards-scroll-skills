# Trend Research Scout

## Purpose

Use this skill to find current, usable content signals and translate them into brand-safe angles. Trend work must be date-aware and source-backed.

## Research Workflow

1. Define the niche and audience:
   - Industry, geography, language, buyer type, sophistication level.
   - Platform priority: Telegram, Instagram, TikTok, or cross-platform.
   - Brand risk tolerance: conservative, sharp, playful, experimental.

2. Gather current signals:
   - Platform search pages, creator posts, competitor accounts, comments, hashtags, audio pages, newsletters, official platform docs, and analytics when available.
   - Prefer fresh sources. Record dates and links.
   - Separate trend, meme, format, topic, and audience pain.

3. Filter:
   - Is this relevant to the user's audience?
   - Is the format repeatable by the team?
   - Does it fit the brand voice?
   - Is it already saturated?
   - Can it be turned into proof, education, opinion, or offer content?

## Trend Card Output

Use:

`signal | source/date | platform | why it matters | brand-safe angle | format | hook | asset needed | risk`

Then recommend:

- 3 low-risk adaptations.
- 3 sharper experiments.
- 1 thing to avoid.

## Rules

- Browse for current information before claiming a trend is live or rising.
- Do not treat a single viral post as proof of a market trend.
- Avoid copying creators. Translate the mechanic, not the content.
- Use `$hook-retention-lab` to improve trend hooks.
- Use `$social-publishing-qa` before publishing trend-led content.
