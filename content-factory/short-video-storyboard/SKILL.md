# Short Video Storyboard

## Purpose

Use this skill to turn a short video idea into a shootable and editable plan. A storyboard is not finished until each script beat has a visible action.

## Inputs To Capture

Clarify or infer:

- Platform and aspect ratio, usually 9:16.
- Target length.
- Face-to-camera, voiceover, b-roll, screen recording, animation, or mixed.
- Available locations, people, product, footage, and props.
- Brand tone and visual style.
- Required CTA.

## Storyboard Structure

Produce:

`timecode | shot | action | voiceover | on-screen text | camera/framing | edit notes | assets needed`

For each beat:

- Keep on-screen text short enough to read.
- Make the first shot concrete and attention-worthy.
- Add b-roll where a talking head would drag.
- Use captions as part of the design, not as an afterthought.
- Include transition notes only when they help the message.

## Production Checks

- Can the team shoot this without asking what to film?
- Does every second earn its place?
- Does the visual change often enough for shortform retention?
- Is the CTA visible and natural?
- Are claims, logos, client names, and music rights safe to use?

If the user needs copy first, use `$tiktok-shortform-script-lab` or `$instagram-reels-carousel-factory` before storyboarding.
