# Social Publishing QA

## Purpose

Use this skill as the final gate before social content is published or handed to a production team.

## QA Checklist

Review:

- Factual accuracy: claims, dates, names, numbers, screenshots, testimonials.
- Source status: mark unsupported claims as `needs source`.
- Platform fit: Telegram depth, Instagram visual packaging, TikTok native pacing.
- Hook strength: first line, cover, first 3 seconds, or slide 1.
- CTA: one clear action, correct link or handoff.
- Brand voice: consistent tone, forbidden words, terminology, offer framing.
- Formatting: mobile readability, line breaks, caption length, slide density.
- Accessibility: subtitles, alt text when needed, readable contrast and text size.
- Rights: music, footage, faces, logos, client names, creator attribution.
- Compliance: regulated claims, guarantees, medical/legal/financial advice, platform policy risk.
- Production readiness: assets, owner, deadline, approval status.

## Verdicts

Use:

- `approve`: safe to publish.
- `revise`: publish after specific fixes.
- `block`: do not publish until a serious issue is resolved.

## Output Format

Provide:

`verdict | top risks | required fixes | optional improvements | final revised copy if useful`

## Rules

- Be direct and specific.
- Do not rewrite everything when a focused fix is enough.
- Do not approve content with invented proof, unclear rights, or unsafe claims.
- Browse or ask for current sources when platform rules, trend claims, laws, or factual claims could be outdated.
