# TikTok Shortform Script Lab

## Purpose

Use this skill to turn an idea into TikTok concepts that feel immediate, specific, and watchable. Favor raw clarity and human tension over polished brand language.

## Concept Workflow

1. Extract the tension:
   - What belief is wrong?
   - What mistake is common?
   - What result is surprising?
   - What behind-the-scenes detail feels insider?
   - What would make the target viewer say "that is me"?

2. Generate angles:
   - Contrarian take.
   - Personal confession.
   - Fast tutorial.
   - Myth versus reality.
   - Mistake list.
   - Live breakdown.
   - Storytime with a useful point.
   - Duet/stitch/comment reply angle when source material allows.

3. Build the script:
   - 0-3 seconds: hook plus clear visual.
   - 3-10 seconds: tension, problem, or setup.
   - 10-25 seconds: payoff, steps, proof, or reveal.
   - Last beat: loop, comment prompt, or CTA.

## Script Output

Use this structure:

`concept | target viewer | hook | shot/action | voiceover | on-screen text | edit notes | caption | CTA | loop`

Provide 3-7 variants when testing angles.

## Native Rules

- Do not write like a brand manifesto.
- Keep sentences speakable and short.
- Add visible action or changing frames; a script without visuals is unfinished.
- Use concrete nouns, numbers, and situations.
- Avoid fake trends. Browse current sources before referencing sounds, memes, platform behavior, or trend momentum.
- Do not promise virality; optimize for testable hooks and repeatable learning.
