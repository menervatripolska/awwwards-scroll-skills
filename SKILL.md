# Hook Retention Lab

## Purpose

Use this skill to diagnose and rebuild the opening and retention curve of social content. Focus on making the right audience pay attention, not on empty clickbait.

## Diagnose

Score the draft from 1-5 on:

- Specificity: does it name a real situation, audience, or outcome?
- Tension: is there a problem, contradiction, risk, or curiosity gap?
- Speed: does the value appear quickly?
- Visuality: can the opening be seen, not just read?
- Payoff: does the content satisfy the promise?
- Brand fit: does it sound like the user, not a generic growth account?

## Hook Families

Use 5-10 hook variants across different families:

- Problem: "You are not stuck because X. You are stuck because Y."
- Contrarian: "Everyone tells you X. In practice, Y matters more."
- Proof: "We changed X and got Y because Z."
- Mistake: "The expensive mistake is not X. It is Y."
- Speedrun: "Here is the fastest way to understand X."
- Behind the scenes: "What nobody shows about X..."
- Identity: "If you are the kind of person who X, watch/read this."
- Comparison: "X looks similar to Y until this point."

## Retention Beats

Add beats every few seconds or paragraphs:

- Pattern interrupt: switch visual, pace, sentence shape, or proof type.
- Open loop: name what will be revealed soon.
- Micro-payoff: give value before asking for more attention.
- Specific example: replace abstraction with a concrete case.
- Stakes: show what happens if the viewer ignores the idea.
- Loop ending: make the last line connect back to the first.

## Output

Provide:

`diagnosis | best hook | alternate hooks | retention map | revised draft | why it works`

Keep revisions honest. Do not add false urgency, fake scarcity, fake controversy, or unsupported numbers.
