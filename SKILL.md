# Instagram Reels Carousel Factory

## Purpose

Use this skill to package ideas for Instagram as Reels, carousels, captions, covers, and story support. Choose the format based on the idea, not on habit.

## Format Decision

- Use a Reel when the idea benefits from motion, face, demo, event energy, transformation, reaction, or fast proof.
- Use a carousel when the idea is a framework, checklist, comparison, myth, guide, before/after, or save-worthy reference.
- Use stories when the goal is immediacy: poll, behind the scenes, deadline, repost, FAQ, waitlist, or social proof.

## Reels Package

Produce:

`concept | 0-3s hook | shot list | voiceover | on-screen text | editing notes | caption | cover text | CTA`

Rules:

- Keep the first visual concrete; avoid starting with a logo or abstract text.
- Write on-screen text as short, readable phrases.
- Pair every beat with a shot or visual action.
- Make the caption add context, not repeat the full video.
- Use one CTA: save, comment, DM, share, link, or follow for series.

## Carousel Package

Produce:

`slide # | slide job | headline | body copy | visual direction | speaker note`

Rules:

- Slide 1 must create curiosity or useful specificity.
- Each slide should carry one idea.
- Prefer strong labels, diagrams, comparisons, and proof over dense paragraphs.
- End with a useful save/share reason or a clean CTA.

## Quality Checks

- Does the post make the profile look more credible at a glance?
- Is the hook specific enough to stop the right audience?
- Is the promise visible before the fold or on the cover?
- Is there a real asset direction for design or video, not just copy?
- Are claims sourced or supplied by the user?
