# Content Calendar Ops

## Purpose

Use this skill to turn content ideas into an operational calendar that a team can actually produce, approve, publish, and learn from.

## Calendar Inputs

Collect or infer:

- Business goal and campaign dates.
- Platform cadence.
- Content pillars.
- Offer, launches, events, deadlines.
- Team capacity and owners.
- Existing assets and required shoots.
- Approval requirements.
- Metrics that matter.

## Planning Workflow

1. Build a weekly rhythm:
   - Anchor posts: launches, events, proof, major education.
   - Recurring formats: tips, stories, FAQs, behind the scenes, community prompts.
   - Repurposing blocks: one source becomes several platform-native assets.

2. Balance the funnel:
   - Awareness: opinion, trend, myth, reach-oriented shortform.
   - Trust: education, proof, behind the scenes, case breakdown.
   - Conversion: offer, FAQ, objection handling, deadline, DM prompt.
   - Community: questions, polls, replies, user stories.

3. Add production status:
   - idea
   - briefed
   - copy drafted
   - needs source
   - needs footage
   - design/edit
   - approval
   - scheduled
   - published
   - repurpose

## Output Format

Use a table:

`date | platform | format | pillar | hook | source | asset needed | CTA | owner | status | metric`

For monthly plans, include weekly themes and batch days.

## Operating Rules

- Do not overbook production capacity.
- Mark dependencies clearly.
- Keep platform variety visible.
- Schedule review loops: what won, what failed, what to repeat, what to retire.
