# Telegram Channel Writer

## Purpose

Use this skill to write Telegram content that feels like a strong channel, not a recycled Instagram caption. Optimize for trust, clarity, saved messages, replies, and forwardability.

## Workflow

1. Identify the post job:
   - Teach a useful idea.
   - Reframe a painful belief.
   - Share proof or a case.
   - Announce an offer or event.
   - Start discussion.
   - Move readers to a link, DM, form, or next post.

2. Choose a Telegram-native format:
   - Sharp note: one point, one turn, one CTA.
   - Founder memo: opinion, story, lesson, invitation.
   - Checklist: steps, mistakes, or diagnostics.
   - Case breakdown: context, action, result, takeaway.
   - Launch post: problem, promise, proof, deadline, CTA.
   - Series: 3-7 connected posts with a visible arc.

3. Draft structure:
   - First line: direct hook, tension, confession, result, or specific promise.
   - Context: why this matters now.
   - Value: the actual insight, framework, story, or proof.
   - Friction removal: answer the likely objection.
   - CTA: one action only.

## Style Rules

- Use short paragraphs and visible rhythm; Telegram readers scan vertically.
- Avoid generic motivational filler, ad-speak, and over-polished corporate language.
- Make the post sound like a person with taste and stakes.
- Use bullets only when they increase clarity.
- Do not invent numbers, client outcomes, testimonials, screenshots, or market facts.
- If the source is thin, ask for missing proof or write a clearly labeled draft angle.

## Output Format

For single posts, provide:

`title/working angle | final post | CTA | optional pinned comment | variants`

For series, provide:

`post # | job | hook | core idea | CTA | dependency`

When rewriting, include a brief note on what changed: hook, clarity, proof, CTA, or voice.
