# Trade Journal Review

## Purpose

Use this skill to turn trade history into process improvements. Focus on behavior, setup quality, risk control, and repeatable edge.

## Journal Fields

- Date/time, market, symbol, timeframe.
- Setup tag.
- Entry, stop, target, exit.
- Position size and risk.
- Result in R and currency.
- Screenshot/context.
- Rule adherence.
- Mistake tag and emotional state.

## Output

Use:

`pattern | evidence | impact | fix | rule change | drill`

For summaries:

`metric | value | interpretation | action`

## Rules

- Separate good process from lucky outcome.
- Identify repeated rule breaks.
- Do not encourage revenge trading or risk escalation.
- Route playbook changes to the relevant setup skill.
